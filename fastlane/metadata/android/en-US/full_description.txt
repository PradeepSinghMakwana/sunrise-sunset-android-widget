See your local time, sunrise, and sunset at a glance.

Sunrise Sunset Clock is a lightweight Android home-screen widget that calculates daily sunrise and sunset times using your device's approximate location.

Features
• Live digital clock using your device's system time zone.
• Daily sunrise and sunset times for your approximate location.
• Approximate location permission only.
• Offline solar-time calculation after location is available.
• No account, API key, or external location service required.

Privacy
The app requests approximate location only. Location coordinates are stored locally on your device and used for the solar-time calculation. The app does not require precise location access.

Add the widget
Open the app and allow approximate location access. Then add Sunrise Sunset Clock from your Android launcher's Widgets screen.

Accuracy
Solar times use a NOAA-style approximation and may differ slightly from published almanacs. Results depend on the location available to Android.
